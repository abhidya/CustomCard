Terms

CustomCard provides a card-drafting, review, and print-handoff workflow. Customers are responsible for reviewing names, dates, artwork, message text, and order details before downloading print files or continuing to a printer. When you choose "Print at Walgreens," CustomCard uploads your approved card images to Walgreens and opens Walgreens hosted checkout; Walgreens is the merchant for the print order, sets the price, and collects payment. CustomCard never captures or stores payment card details.

  • Do not use CustomCard to create unlawful, harmful, infringing, deceptive, or harassing content.
  • Card drafts are suggestions and may need editing before printing.
  • Retail printer terms apply when a customer separately chooses to upload files to a printer.

Privacy

CustomCard is designed to minimize relationship data collection. The current app can run locally in the browser, keeps optional tracking off by default, and treats relationship memories as reviewable customer inputs. Account-backed features may use authentication, draft state, memory review, and support data needed to provide the service. If you connect Google Calendar, CustomCard reads upcoming event titles and dates with a read-only scope, stores only the event title, date, and timezone, and encrypts your refresh credential; CustomCard does not use Google Calendar data for advertising and does not transfer it to third parties except as needed to provide the card workflow you requested. Card details you enter (names, notes, occasion) are shared with the service providers needed to run the product — for example our hosting, authentication, and database providers, the AI providers that draft card text and artwork when you use AI drafting, and Walgreens when you start a print order.

  • Relationship notes should be entered only with appropriate permission and reviewed before use.
  • Calendar or contact imports should use scoped, revocable OAuth access when enabled.
  • Data access, correction, deletion, and consent records require the hosted request workflow before launch.

Cookies

Necessary cookies or browser storage may support sign-in, security, theme preference, and local draft workflow state. Optional analytics and marketing trackers are not part of the default repo behavior and must stay blocked until consent tooling and evidence are configured.

  • Necessary storage supports account, security, and product operation.
  • Analytics storage requires consent and proof that scripts wait for that consent.
  • Marketing storage requires opt-in, unsubscribe, and suppression-list evidence.

Refunds

CustomCard does not capture payment. Print orders placed through Walgreens hosted checkout — and any manually uploaded print files — are transactions between you and Walgreens. Any refund, cancellation, pickup, shipping, or print-quality issue for a print order is governed by Walgreens (or the printer you chose) and its policies.

  • CustomCard itself never charges you; payment happens at Walgreens checkout.
  • Manual handoff files should be proofread before upload to a printer.
  • Future paid checkout requires payment, refund, dispute, and vendor-policy review evidence.

AI disclosure

CustomCard may assist with card copy, art direction, and panel concepts. AI-assisted output can be inaccurate, generic, or visually unsuitable for print, so customers and operators must review text, names, dates, personal details, and imagery before use. Live provider calls remain gated by admin configuration and safety evidence.

  • AI-assisted content should not be treated as professional, legal, medical, or financial advice.
  • Human review is required before print, public sharing, or customer delivery.
  • Unsafe-output review, prompt audit, and provider approval evidence are required before production rollout.

Privacy choices

Customers should be able to request access, correction, deletion, memory removal, provider disconnect, and consent changes. The repository tracks these obligations, but production launch still requires a public intake path, identity verification workflow, retention review, and opt-out evidence.

  • Use support or account settings to request relationship-memory removal when available.
  • Do not enter sensitive third-party details unless they are needed for the card workflow.
  • Global Privacy Control and sale/share opt-out behavior must be verified before marketing or analytics launch.
