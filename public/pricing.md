# Pricing — CustomCard

Last updated: June 2026

## Summary

CustomCard is free to use. You pay the print shop only when you actually print a card. CustomCard itself does not charge for creating, editing, or downloading cards.

---

## Free tier

- Price: $0 — no credit card required
- Account: optional to start; required for AI generation and saved history
- What's included:
  - AI-assisted card drafting (words and artwork)
  - 5 × 7 folded card layout (four panels: front, inside-left, inside-right, back)
  - Print-ready PDF/PNG download at 300 DPI
  - All card occasions: birthday, graduation, wedding, anniversary, thank-you, sympathy, friendship, belated
  - Manual invite paste or .ics calendar file import
  - Card history (requires free account)
  - Relationship memory for future personalization (requires free account)
  - Google Calendar connection for automatic occasion detection (requires free account)
  - Multilingual and right-to-left script support

## What you pay for (at the print shop)

CustomCard prepares your print-ready files. You choose a print shop and pay them directly for printing and pickup or delivery. CustomCard is not involved in that transaction.

Typical retail print shop prices (estimates only — confirm with your chosen vendor):

| Vendor | 5×7 greeting card (flat or folded) | Pickup time |
|--------|-------------------------------------|-------------|
| Walgreens Photo | ~$3–5 per card | Same day (1–2 hrs typical) |
| CVS Photo | ~$3–5 per card | Same day |
| FedEx Office | ~$4–7 per card | Same day or next day |
| Local print shops | Varies | Varies |

CustomCard surfaces a fulfillment comparison (lowest estimate, fastest pickup) but does not place orders or handle payment.

## FAQ

**Is there a subscription?**
No. CustomCard does not charge a subscription fee. Creating and downloading cards is always free.

**When would I pay?**
Only when you take your print-ready files to a print shop and confirm the order with that vendor.

**Does CustomCard handle payment?**
No. Payment goes directly to your chosen print shop (Walgreens, CVS, FedEx Office, etc.). CustomCard is not in that transaction.

**Is there a premium plan with more features?**
Not currently for consumer use. Business lifecycle card features are available on request.

**Can I try it without an account?**
Yes. You can start a card, see the AI draft, and preview the layout without an account. Saving your card, enabling AI generation, and connecting your calendar require a free account.
